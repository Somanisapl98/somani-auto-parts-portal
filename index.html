ABOUT US

LEADING CENTRAL INDIA COMMERCIAL VEHICLE PARTS & OILS DISTRIBUTOR

Somani Auto Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, has been a trusted pillar of the commercial vehicle aftermarket for over 27 years. We deal exclusively in Commercial Vehicle Spare Parts, Automotive Oils & Lubricants, and Hard-to-Find Illiquid Stock.

Supported by 10 strategically positioned branches across Chhattisgarh and Madhya Pradesh and direct partnerships with Tata Motors, Cummins, Holset, JOST, RSB, SKF Bearing, DRiV, and Uno Minda, we deliver rapid availability for heavy trucks, tippers, buses, and fleet operators.
