ABOUT US

LEADING COMMERCIAL VEHICLE PARTS, OILS & ILLIQUID STOCK PARTNER

Somani Auto Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, brings 27+ years of experience dedicated exclusively to Commercial Vehicle Spare Parts, Automotive Oils & Lubricants, and Illiquid Stock.

Operating across 10 branches in Chhattisgarh and Madhya Pradesh alongside Tata Motors, Cummins, Holset, JOST, RSB, SKF Bearing, DRiV, and Uno Minda, we supply genuine parts and bulk fleet lubricants with dependable regional reach.
